Home Chef is a Chicago-based meal kit and food delivery company that delivers pre-portioned ingredients and recipes to subscribers weekly in the United States.  Homechef delivers three million meals monthly to its subscribers.

I work with the Homechef marketing and design teams to build landing pages for their marketing campaigns. Using Webflow Enterprise, I've built 50+ landing pages for the team and they get millions of page visits per month.

I worked on their collaboration pages with brands like **Star Wars, Bluey, Spongebob, Minecraft, Kroger**, etc. I also help maintain their Webflow site, making updates to pages, fixing bugs and making sure things run smoothly.
